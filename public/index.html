        About The Winning Shot

        Three friends. Every game. Graded Tuesday.

        The Winning Shot is Hundo, Jon and Collin picking every NFL game straight up. The sheet goes up before kickoff, the green pen comes out when the games are final, and the season standings never reset.

        How the sheet works

        • SEACircleOne of us went against the other two.
        • JAXCheckThe pick was right.
        • CLEStrikeThe pick was wrong.
        • HUNDOUnderlineLeads the season.
        • Question markNothing was submitted.

        The rules

        Nothing is invented

        Every score and record on this site comes from a real final result. When something is wrong, the correction goes up as loudly as the mistake.

        Everyone gets named

        All three of us appear on every sheet at the same size, including the weeks one of us is the story.

        Straight up

        Pick the winner. No spreads. A tie counts for nobody.

        Work with us

        Brands, shows and athletes: tell us what you have in mind and we'll reply by email.
